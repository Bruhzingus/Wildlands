# Phase 15 implementation and verification

Implemented 2026-10-20 in the local Babylon/TypeScript game.

PHASE: 15 — New maps, bosses and replay hooks

What changed: Expanded every map from 230 × 230 m to 287.5 × 287.5 m: **25% more width and depth, 56.25% more area**. Playable hard boundaries, soft boundaries, terrain, scenery distribution, boundary decoration, enemy arrival bounds and minimap projection use the expanded dimensions. Eighteen discoveries are initially available instead of twelve. Six outer discoveries vary in kind as well as position; existing free caches, all three paid chest tiers, shrine trials, springs and elite encounters remain available. Three more discoveries emerge at 3:00, 7:00 and 11:00: a healing spring, a free cache and an optional champion. Availability, announcements, interaction, mesh visibility and minimap markers use simulation time and freeze during menus. Sites remain optional; standing near spawn still supports a full run.

Added `MapDefinition`, `WorldProfile`, `EncounterDefinition` and `ChallengeDefinition` registries. The seed now controls quarter-turn orientation, lake position, hill location/relief, discovery placement/types and scenery. Rendering, collision, region labels and the minimap share the same profile. A small visibility graph routes trails around the actual shifted lake. Authored landmark geometry is map data, and the same primitives supply rendering and colliders. Fixed Pinewood/Ruins routes keep their seed IDs and repeatable layouts within this version; their geography intentionally changes from phase 14 with the expansion.

Added **Volcanic Ridge**, available immediately in the title selector for fresh and migrated saves. It has Ashen Clearing, Ember Grove, Basalt Ridge and Crater Shore, a warm environment palette, taller basalt formations, Ash Spire/Cinder Gate/Sun Stones/Crater Lake landmarks, stronger ridge relief, more rock scenery, and Fire/Rock encounter weighting. Weighted draws retain the normal time-tier species pool as a fallback. Its optional guardians and reinforcements use its own species lists. **Moltres** arrives at 15:00 with an aimed nine-shot Ember Fan, a slower expanding Solar Flare wave and Ashen Flock reinforcements, in a distinct four-step cycle. These reuse the existing committed telegraphs, projectiles, jump/dodge wave avoidance and budgets. Mewtwo retains its three-pattern encounter on the other maps. Boss names, warning text, attacks, wave colors and victory UI follow the selected encounter. Both encounters retain victory and optional endless with the same party/build.

Added two optional mutations: **Onslaught**, 25% more frequent arrivals (interval ×0.8), and **Volatile**, 25% more enemy damage, including boss contact/volleys/waves and shrine damage floors. Both are available immediately alongside Standard. Existing Quickened and No healing achievement gates remain intact. Save versions 1/2 keep the same key/schema, preserve collection and earned choices, and receive the new base choices during migration. Legendary victory accomplishments accept either map boss, with stable achievement IDs and no permanent stat rewards.

Why the approach is maintainable: Map/encounter/challenge content is data; movement, combat, recruitment, upgrades and progression remain shared. Maps select encounter definitions rather than adding species-specific combat branches. New palettes, authored landmark primitives, spawn preferences, boss sequences, attack tuning and timed optional sites can be added in the registries without editing Simulation combat logic. A shared world profile prevents terrain/render/minimap disagreement. Mutation multipliers are centralized. Discovery state uses the existing POI/reward paths and one authoritative run clock; there are no independent timers or mandatory quest state machines.

Known limitations: There are two environment families and two repeatable routes, not four unrelated worlds. Volcanic Ridge deliberately reuses the primitive/biome-layout kit; its theme, relief, landmark compositions, weighting and boss differ. During-run map changes reveal optional discoveries; terrain/colliders do not rearrange beneath actors. Fixed route layouts differ from previous releases. No human balance session or target-hardware GPU benchmark was conducted, and scripted browser fixtures do not establish a natural fifteen-minute victory rate. No deployment was performed.

Balance / performance impact: Initial discoveries increase 50%, with three later opportunities; the two original free caches remain, outer paid caches recycle the three existing rarity tiers, and the timed cache is free. Exploration offers more supplies/trials without increasing baseline enemy budgets or changing recruitment/evolution time gates. The two new difficulty rules are opt-in. The prop cap rises from 610 to 820, with source mesh/material reuse; terrain subdivisions rise from 92 to 116 to retain ground detail over the larger area. Enemy/projectile/drop/effect caps and obstacle spatial indexing remain in force. Resource tests verify cleanup and geometry sharing, not target-machine FPS. Human tuning should evaluate exploration travel times, extra supply density, Moltres pacing and Onslaught pressure.
